ABOUT 2-METER AMATEUR BAND SOURCES

The 2-meter band (144.000 to 148.000 MHz) is the most popular VHF amateur radio spectrum in the world. It provides reliable local line-of-sight communications, repeater linking, amateur satellite relays, and severe weather SKYWARN networks.

How Internet Streaming of 2M Works

  • Repeater Gateways & Icecast: Radio clubs wire their repeater base stations to dedicated sound cards and stream live audio via Icecast or Broadcastify servers.
  • WebSDR / OpenWebRX: Software-Defined Radios attached to VHF antennas stream wideband IQ audio spectrum directly across the internet.
  • EchoLink & IRLP: VoIP systems enabling hams to cross-connect repeaters across continents.
  • ISS VHF Crossband (145.800 MHz): Downlink signals from astronauts and crossband voice repeaters on the International Space Station.

Transceiver Audio Engine

This app combines real live streams with an authentic FM discriminator noise generator. When squelch is open or when tuning between stations, realistic atmospheric RF noise is simulated using the Web Audio API. Pressing the PTT MIC simulates keying up with a classic Motorola/NASA Quindar roger beep!
